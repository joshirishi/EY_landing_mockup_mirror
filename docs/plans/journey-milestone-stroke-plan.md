# Journey milestone lower-stroke plan

## What this is

At the bottom of the Ascent journey map there are **three phrase boxes**:

1. From uncertainty to impact
2. From learning to leading
3. From user to AI-enabled tax professional

Each box can show a **yellow underline**. That line is meant to mean: **this phase of the journey is complete**.

## How it works today

- The yellow trail on the mountain and the three underlines follow the same “how far have we walked?” setting.
- Clicking a camp or opening a module can move that setting, so the underlines grow or shrink.
- On **module 1.1** (start of the trek), none of the three phases are finished yet, so all three underlines stay empty. That can look like a broken or unfinished bar.
- The boxes themselves stay equally bright, so an empty underline is easy to notice and easy to misread.

## The decision

There are two different intents. We should pick one as the default:

1. **Phase-complete badges** — the underline is a trophy: “you finished this stretch of the mountain.”
2. **Live course progress** — the underline (and maybe a pulse) tracks where the learner is *right now*.

**Recommended default: phase-complete badges first (Option A).**  
Later, if you want real tracking, add **remember progress + you-are-here** (B + C). Do not mix both meanings on the same line without a label.

## Recommended plan (phased)

### Phase 1 — Clarify (smallest)

- Add a short label so people know the boxes are **phase milestones**, not a loading bar.
- Dim or soften boxes that have not been reached yet, so an empty underline reads as “not yet,” not “broken.”
- Likely pieces to touch: the bottom banner and the three `MilestoneBox` cards on the Ascent journey map (also reused from the prompting page’s journey progress section).
- **Success:** on module 1.1, empty strokes look intentional.

### Phase 2 — Don’t lose progress on click

- On a **module page**, lock the banner to that module’s place on the trail so clicking around does not wipe the underlines.
- The **hub** can stay clickable for exploration, **or** lock after the first open — choose one so people are not surprised.

### Phase 3 — Real progress (if you choose it)

- Save which modules are finished in the browser (stays on this computer until cleared).
- Hub and every module page should show the **same** completed phases.
- Optional: a “you are here” treatment on the **current** phase (partial line or a gentle pulse) — only if we are doing live progress, not badges-only.

### Phase 4 — Optional finer mapping

- Add more checkpoints only if three boxes feel too static once progress is real.

## Out of scope

Do not rebuild the whole trek map, path art, or camp layout. This plan is only the **three bottom boxes** and what their yellow underlines mean.

## Open questions for you

- Do you want **badges** (phase complete) or **live progress**?
- On the hub, should clicking camps turn the yellow lines **off** again, or should completed lines stay on?
- On module **1.1**, should the first box show as **in progress** (partial / pulse), or stay empty until that whole phase is done?
