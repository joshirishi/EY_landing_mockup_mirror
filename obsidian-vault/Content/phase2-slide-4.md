# Slide 4 — Do Not Begin with "We Need an Agent"

**Section:** PROBLEM FIRST  
**Subtitle:** A technology choice should follow the problem definition—not precede it.

---

## 01 — What work is being performed?
- Tax process and key activities
- Trigger and required output

## 02 — Where does effort or friction arise?
- Searching, reviewing, comparing, drafting
- Follow-ups, tracking, evidence and reporting

## 03 — What makes the activity difficult?
- Volume, stakeholders and repositories
- Formats, hand-offs, missing information and judgment

## 04 — What should improve?
- Effort, speed and consistency
- Visibility, evidence organisation and time for analysis

---

## Workshop prompt

**Which tax processes consume the most effort today—and which individual activities create that effort?**
