# Slide 8 — Your Tax Process. Your Pain Points. Your Opportunities.

**Section:** LIVE BRAINSTORM  
**Subtitle:** EY's guided samples open the conversation. The client's validated process and pain points determine the opportunity.

---

## A — PROCESS
- Process selected
- Business objective
- Trigger and final output
- Frequency

## B — CURRENT ACTIVITIES
- Key steps and owners
- Documents and data
- Systems and repositories

## C — FRICTION
- Time and follow-ups
- Repeated searching
- Errors, inconsistencies or delays
- Professional judgment points

## D — OPPORTUNITY
- Prompt
- M365 Agent
- Prompt + Agent
- Process improvement
- Human-led activity

## E — CONTROL QUESTIONS
- Permitted information and sources
- Qualified reviewer
- Stop or escalation points
- What remains human-led

## F — INITIAL PRIORITY
- Relevance and friction
- Repeatability
- Inputs and sources
- Human review
- Practicality

---

> **Select one tax process. Map the activities. Name the friction. Then choose the response—Prompt, Agent, both, process change or human-led.**
