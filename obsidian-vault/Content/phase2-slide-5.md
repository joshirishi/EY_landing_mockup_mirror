# Slide 5 — EY-Guided Prompt Examples

**Section:** GUIDED EXAMPLES  
**Subtitle:** Purpose, approach and outcome as summarised in Sheet1 of Sample use cases.xlsx. These examples stimulate discussion; they are not a preselected implementation list.

---

## Prompt Examples

| PROMPT NAME | PURPOSE | APPROACH | OUTCOME |
|-------------|---------|----------|---------|
| **Concept Note** | Reviewing research from multiple sources and helps draft requisite concept note for larger consumption | Analyses tax research data collated from various platforms to draft a simplified concept note in line with requirements listed and preferred drafting style | Creates a precise and informative summary reflecting tax issue discussed, conclusions, assumptions, and key guidance provided. |
| **Facts-to-Law Mapping** | Review of large agreements with multiple clauses and bulky landmark tax judgements, to identify relevant tax, regulatory and compliance implications. | Evaluate fact patterns against bulky landmark judgements, to identify clauses impacted by the said judgement against relevant laws, treaty provisions and legal requirements | Produces a structured comparative summary and/ or advisory memo, highlighting relevant clauses from case law and agreement and highlights key issues, tax implications, risks and practical recommendations. |
| **20-80 Concept Simplification** | Accelerates understanding of a new tax or technical topic by focusing on essential 20% knowledge to comprehend 80% of topic breadth | Separates foundational principles from advanced topics and explains their significance in a structured format. | Delivers a concise learning guide that enables quicker understanding and more informed discussions. |
| **Stepwise Concept Plan** | Simplifies complex tax, regulatory or business topics for easier learning and stakeholder communication. | Breaks a topic into logical building blocks supported by examples, analogies and progressive explanations. | Creates an easy-to-follow roadmap that makes complex concepts more relatable and actionable. |
| **Transaction Step Plan** | Supports planning and execution of transactions, restructuring projects and regulatory processes. | Identifies key activities, timelines, responsibilities, documentation requirements and relevant legal provisions. | Generates an implementation tracker and execution roadmap to improve project governance and completion. |
| **GST Formula Validation** | Automates validation of GST-related data used in compliance and reporting processes. | Generates Excel formulas to test GSTINs, invoice numbers and HSN/SAC codes against specified validation rules. | Improves data quality, reduces manual review effort and enhances reporting accuracy. |
| **Meeting Minutes** | Converts meeting transcripts and discussion notes into structured records and action trackers. | Captures agendas, executive summaries, speaker-wise discussions, decisions and assigned responsibilities. | Produces professional meeting minutes with clear actions, ownership and next steps. |
| **PPT Mock Run** | Helps presenters draft presentations and prepare for leadership, client and internal meetings. | Drafts and Reviews slide content, develops speaking notes, identifies key messages and anticipates audience questions. | Improves presentation quality, confidence, storytelling and audience engagement. |
| **Tax Strategic Upskilling** | Supports capability building and professional development across tax and technology domains. | Creates structured learning plans incorporating practical exercises, resources, milestones and study schedules. | Delivers a tailored upskilling roadmap to accelerate learning and long-term capability development. |
| **Document Extraction** | Extracts relevant information from tax and financial documents and converts it into structured data. | Applies predefined extraction rules, mappings and validation criteria against uploaded source documents. | Creates standardized datasets that can be readily used for compliance, reporting and analysis. |
| **Image Summarization** | Converts handwritten notes, scanned content and images into usable business information. | Extracts text, identifies visual elements and summarizes key observations and discussion points. | Produces structured summaries, meeting notes and key takeaways without manual transcription effort. |
| **Vernacular Translation** | Enables review of tax, legal and regulatory documents issued in regional languages - for example WHT certificates, invoices, credit notes etc., | Extracts and translates the complete document, including tables and supporting content, while preserving meaning and structure. | Delivers an accurate English version suitable for analysis, compliance and decision-making. |
| **VBA Automation** | Automates repetitive spreadsheet and file-management activities through VBA-based solutions. | Converts business requirements into VBA scripts with supporting implementation instructions. | Reduces manual effort, improves consistency and enables scalable automation of routine tasks. |
| **Agreement Review** | Reviews agreements to identify commercial, tax and compliance considerations. | Evaluates contractual provisions, tax implications, reporting obligations and drafting inconsistencies. | Provides a structured review highlighting risks, observations and recommendations to strengthen tax position. |
| **SOP Review** | Assesses policies, procedures and operating frameworks for potential improvements. | Tests existing frameworks against realistic scenarios, risks and considerations. | Produces actionable recommendations to improve policy effectiveness. |
