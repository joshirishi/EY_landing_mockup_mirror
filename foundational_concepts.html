Think Beyond Drafting and Summarisation

Generative AI can be your smart thought partner. Tap a capability to see it in action.

Create

Produce new content from your instructions.

“Draft a response to a tax authority notice based on the facts provided”

“Convert this GST reconciliation table into a user-ready summary.”

“Combine the judgment, circular and statutory provisions into a consolidated briefing note.”

Understand

Make sense of information that already exists.

“What is the time limit for filing a revised return?”

“Summarize this tax judgment in five key takeaways.”

“Explain POEM in simple language for a CFO presentation.”

Organize

Structure and compare what you already have.

“Extract TDS amounts, PANs and section references from Form 26AS.”

“Classify expenses as capital or revenue based on the descriptions provided.”

“Compare the India–Singapore and India–Mauritius treaties for capital gains taxation.”

Think

Weigh judgment calls and reason toward a recommendation.

“Analyze these transactions and highlight potential transfer pricing risk areas.”

“Evaluate the risks and support available for this tax position.”

“Suggest factors to consider when evaluating a cross-border restructuring.”

2 — AI AGENT

AI Agent

What is added?
LLM Capability
Access to tools
Ability to take actions
What it does

Not only answers — but also does things.

Think of it as

A virtual team member.

Example

Instead of:

Summarize this GST notice

The Agent:

— reads the notice
— extracts key facts
— drafts a summary
— stores it in the correct folder
Best Used When
—Tasks are repetitive
—Multiple actions are required
—The process is still relatively simple
New Capability Introduced
Takes action
3 — AGENTIC AI

Agentic AI

What is added?
LLM
Tools
Tools
Planning
Decision-making
Multi-step workflow management
What it does

Manages an entire objective from start to finish.

Think of it as

A project manager.

Example

Goal:

Prepare an initial response package for a GST notice.

Agentic AI can:

1. Review the notice
2. Identify due dates
3. Gather supporting documents
4. Draft response points
5. Create a review package
Best Used When
—Multiple steps are involved
—Decisions need to be made
—Several tasks must be coordinated
New Capability Introduced
Plans and coordinates work
